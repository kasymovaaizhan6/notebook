Kyrgyz cuisine grew from nomadic life on the Central Asian steppe — hearty, nourishing food built around lamb, horse meat, flat breads, and fermented dairy. Meat is boiled, not fried; dough is hand-rolled; meals are shared from a common dish. These two recipes are the heart of any Kyrgyz table.

The national dish — boiled lamb over wide noodles with onion broth
